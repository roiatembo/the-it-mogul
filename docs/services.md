# The IT Mogul — Services

## 1. Service philosophy

The IT Mogul does not sell technology for its own sake.

Every service should connect technology to a business problem or outcome.

The service offering can be summarized as:

**Build it. Fix it. Improve it. Automate it. Maintain it.**

The website should make it clear that these services can be purchased independently or combined into a larger project.

For example:

- A business may need only a website.
- A business may need a website + SEO.
- A business may need an existing WordPress site fixed.
- A business may need an online store + payment integration.
- A business may need custom software + business automation.
- A business may need ongoing maintenance after a project.

Do not force customers into predefined packages unless there is a clear business reason.

- Landing pages

- Custom WordPress websites

- Custom functionality
- Forms and lead capture

- Multiple disconnected systems

- Admin systems
- Booking systems

# 10. Service page requirements

Every service page should answer:

### What is it?

Explain the service in plain language.

### Who needs it?

Identify the businesses or situations where it makes sense.

### What problem does it solve?

Describe recognizable problems.

### What do we actually do?

Explain the work clearly.

### What does the customer get?

Describe tangible outcomes.

### Why The IT Mogul?

Explain relevant technical capability and approach.

### What does it cost?

Provide pricing guidance where appropriate without creating artificial fixed prices for work that requires discovery.

### What happens next?

Provide a clear CTA.

---

# 11. Service copy rules

Avoid generic copy such as:

"We deliver cutting-edge solutions to help businesses thrive in the digital landscape."

Prefer specific language such as:

"We build websites, online stores and software that help businesses sell, operate and grow."

Service descriptions should prioritize clarity over cleverness.

Do not fill pages with unnecessary technical terminology.

Technical details should appear where they help establish credibility or explain a solution.

---

# 12. Cross-selling

Cross-selling should be contextual rather than aggressive.

For example:

A Website Development page may naturally recommend:

- SEO
- E-commerce
- Maintenance

A WordPress Troubleshooting page may recommend:

- Maintenance
- Performance optimization
- Custom development

A Custom Software page may recommend:

- Business automation
- API integrations
- Maintenance

Do not add unrelated service promotions simply to increase page length.
